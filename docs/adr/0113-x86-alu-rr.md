# ADR-0113: the integer ALU register/register family in the `opcode` direction

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-09
- Settles: `X86-P81` (the fifteen ALU register/register rows and their byte
  vector)
- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none
- Revises: `EC-42` of `02_encoding_choices.md` (widened from `sub` to the six
  `opcode`/`opcode+1` pairs); the reading-aid table and `0.75.0` paragraph of
  `07_definition_format.md` §8.1; the x86 table of `06_validation_matrix.md`;
  the M6 planning counts of `10_m6_plan.md`; adds
  `definitions/x86_64/0.75.0.json`, `EXP-0112` and the
  `X86-P81`/`N01eu`/`N01ev`/`N01ew`/`N02dd`/`N14ch` fixtures

## Context

`0.74.0` has a partial integer ALU surface: `add` has only its three 64-bit
immediate rows, `sub` has those plus the `29 /r` register/register rows at three
widths, and `and`/`or`/`xor`/`cmp` have no rows at all (form-id scan of
`definitions/x86_64/0.74.0.json`: `add` 3, `sub` 6, no other ALU mnemonic).

Every ALU mnemonic has the same two-opcode direction pair:

| Mnemonic | `opcode` (r/m destination) | `opcode+1` (reg destination) |
|---|---|---|
| `add` | `01` | `03` |
| `or` | `09` | `0B` |
| `and` | `21` | `23` |
| `sub` | `29` | `2B` |
| `xor` | `31` | `33` |
| `cmp` | `39` | `3B` |

At register/register both are legal and `llvm-mc` picks the first. `EXP-0112`
decodes both members of every pair and shows the same instruction text:

- `48 01 c1` and `48 03 c8` are both `addq %rax, %rcx`;
- `48 09 c1`/`48 0b c8`, `48 21 c1`/`48 23 c8`, `48 31 c1`/`48 33 c8`
  and `48 39 c1`/`48 3b c8` are the same for `or`, `and`, `xor` and `cmp`;
- `48 29 c1`/`48 2b c8` is the `sub` pair that `ADR-0110` already measured.

The choice is a byte-affecting decision, so `EC-42` names the direction and the
plan carries the row, never a flag. `0.75.0` adds only the `opcode` direction.

## Decision

1. **Fifteen new rows.** `0.75.0` appends one row per mnemonic and width:
   - `add` at 64/32/16 bits (`4801c0`, `01c0`, `6601c0`);
   - `and` (`4821c0`, `21c0`, `6621c0`), `or` (`4809c0`, `09c0`, `6609c0`),
     `xor` (`4831c0`, `31c0`, `6631c0`) and `cmp` (`4839c0`, `39c0`,
     `6639c0`) at the same three widths.

   This is one row short of the M6 plan's 16-row target. The plan assumed a
   64-bit register/register `add`/`sub` pair; the table shows `sub` already has
   all three widths (`ADR-0110`) and `add` has none. The measured count is 15.
2. **The naming follows the `sub` precedent.** `x86:<mnemonic>/rm<width>-r<width>/legacy`
   puts the destination in `ModRM.r/m` and the source in `ModRM.reg`. For
   `cmp` the first operand is the compared value and is not written; the slot
   keeps the `dest` role because the role set is closed and the encoding role,
   not the write effect, is what the form records.
3. **Slots are the measured ones.** On the 64-bit row the destination is
   `REX.B` at `[0,1]` plus `ModRM.r/m` at `[16,3]`, and the source is `REX.R`
   at `[2,1]` plus `ModRM.reg` at `[19,3]`. On the 32-bit row the fields are
   `[8,3]`/`[11,3]`; on the 16-bit row they are `[16,3]`/`[19,3]` behind the
   `0x66` byte. `REX.W` and `0x66` are template content.
4. **The 64-bit rows reach the high registers; the 32/16-bit rows do not.**
   `%r8`-`%r15` is represented by `REX.B`/`REX.R` on the 64-bit row, so that
   row covers all sixteen registers. The 32/16-bit rows have no `REX` byte, so
   register `8` there is `E-OPERAND-004` (`N14ch`). The high-register refusal
   for the 32/16-bit windows stays deferred.
5. **The `opcode+1` direction stays absent.** No `r64-rm64` row is added,
   including at register/register; `N01ev` names it against `0.75.0` and
   `EC-42` records the choice. The memory shapes, the 8-bit widths, `adc`/`sbb`,
   `test`, `neg`/`not` and the high window of the 32/16-bit rows stay out.
6. **`0.75.0` is additive and immutable.** It repeats the 564 `0.74.0` forms
   byte-for-byte, and the generator asserts the reproduction before it writes
   the new version. The digest is
   `sha256:5b13b6b96a4061f354e7d0a371175936cb66c71dbfa80a7eabbf74bfe7eef40d`.

## Consequences

- `X86-P81-alu-rr` is the 80-byte vector of thirty instructions: each of the
  fifteen rows appears in both operand orders, so the two fields are shown to
  be independent. The input hash is
  `0ec1ea126c3eff49533f940e0fa1b913ff5c75a256fa4fa3fee86f00544a0c38` and the
  output hash is
  `7a657f089b0d339001105fb9678ad64d298624751c291c3bda0861e34e62f34e`.
- `N01eu` (`adc` absent), `N01ev` (the `opcode+1` sibling), `N01ew` (a
  `0.75.0` row against `0.74.0`) are `E-FORM-001`; `N02dd` is
  `E-OPERAND-002` (`expected: 2`, `found: 1`) and `N14ch` is
  `E-OPERAND-004` (`field_bits: 3`).
- The x86 table grows to 579 forms. The corpus grows to 122 positives and 591
  negatives, and every form in `0.75.0` is named by a positive fixture
  (`check_definition.py`).
- `0.74.0` remains readable and unchanged, so a plan pinned to it cannot name
  the new rows and keeps its old byte behaviour.
- The round's planned total becomes 83 new rows and 842 forms, not 84/843.
  `ADR-0112`'s planning target was explicitly not frozen; this ADR records the
  measured count and `10_m6_plan.md` follows it.
  Amended 2026-11-10: `ADR-0115` removes the plan's `REX.W B8+rd id` row (that
  opcode form is `movabs`, `io`), so the round total is now 82 new rows and
  841 forms. This ADR's own S1 count is unchanged.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Add both directions (`01` and `03`) as rows.** Rejected: every assembler
  emits `01`, and two rows for one instruction invite a selector at the
  planner boundary. `EC-42` keeps the choice explicit and the sibling absent.
- **One row with a `direction` modifier.** Rejected: the opcode byte is
  template content and a modifier must not write a template byte.
- **Add all six mnemonics at 8 bits too.** Rejected: the byte-register windows
  (`%al`-`%bl`, `%ah`-`%dh`, `%spl`-`%dil`, `%r8b`-`%r15b`) are a separate
  prefix-window decision and need their own vector.
- **Add the memory shapes now.** Rejected: `ModRM.mod != 11` adds `SIB` and
  displacement geometry; `S4` is the single memory slice of this round.
- **Copy the `sub` opcodes for the other mnemonics.** Rejected: each mnemonic
  has its own opcode byte, and `EXP-0112` measures every pair rather than
  inferring one from another.

## Review triggers

- A memory shape (`ModRM.mod != 11`) is proposed; it adds `SIB`/displacement
  decisions and a different length.
- The `opcode+1` direction is proposed; it needs its own rows and byte vector.
- An 8-bit width is proposed; the byte-register windows change the prefix and
  need their own rows.
- `adc`/`sbb`, `test`, `neg`/`not` or another ALU mnemonic is proposed; the
  opcode byte changes and must be measured.
- The high window of the 32/16-bit rows is proposed; it needs `REX` bytes that
  the current rows do not carry.
